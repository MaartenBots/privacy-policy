Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy covers the following Android games by Maarten Bots:

  • Evolva (com.evolva.game)
  • Life Below (mars.lifebelow)
  • Greenhouse Factory (greenhouse.factory)

Short version

The games do not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases.

What the games store

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Internet use

Life Below and Greenhouse Factory do not use the internet at all.

Evolva works offline, but when your device is online it loads its text fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website request, this means Google receives your device's IP address and basic browser information. Evolva does not send anything else. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Permissions

  • Evolva: internet access, only for loading the fonts described above.
  • Greenhouse Factory: vibration, for short haptic feedback during play.
  • Life Below: no special permissions.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children either.

Google Play

Google Play may collect information when you download or update an app, such as crash reports you agree to share. That is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by these games.

Changes

If a game ever starts collecting data (for example by adding ads or purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
